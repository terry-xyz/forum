# Author Deletion
## Topic
Only the original author may delete their own forum content.

## JTBD
- When I want to remove content I created, I want the forum to let me delete only my own post or comment.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A post author can delete their own post.
- A comment author can delete their own comment.
- A user cannot delete content created by another user.
- A signed-out visitor who tries to delete content is redirected to login before the delete completes.
- Deleted content no longer appears in normal forum views.

## Out Of Scope
- Moderator or staff deletion powers.
- Restoring deleted content.
