# Author Display Snapshot
## Topic
Published content keeps the author display captured at creation time.

## JTBD
- When account details change later, I want published content to keep the author display that was shown when it was created so the historical record stays accurate.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A published post continues to show the author display captured at creation time after later account display changes.
- A published comment continues to show the author display captured at creation time after later account display changes.
- Content created after an account display change shows the newer display captured at its own creation time.
- The preserved author display remains stable on user-facing views where that post or comment author is shown.

## Out Of Scope
- Editing published posts or comments.
- Changing how accounts are displayed at the time new content is created.
- Moderation or deletion behavior.
